Hello New Volunteer

Welcome to the Eden Animal Haven training oreintation. As a volunteer you will have to aid the coordinators in helping them care for the cats there and keep the shelter in good condition. In this oreintation, you will learn how our shelter works and how to properly take care for our fellow cats. this will include:

-Information about the fellow volunteers in this organization

-the various tasks that will need to be done at our shelter

-the proper tips for caring for the cats as needed

-the different behavior type of our cats and how to handle those behaviors

-how to properly socialize with the cats

-and various taks that help in keeping the shelter clean and organized
