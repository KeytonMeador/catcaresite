cat care

The Next portion of your responsibilities will be caring for the cats and learning about their behavior. Learning about how to properly care for the cats beyond the basic five will ensure they are physically and mentally healthy as they await their new owner. The responsibilities tied to caring with the cats will go as follows:

Food - feeding the cats has already been discussed in the basic five. But along with getting their portions right, giving them the right food will also be important. The cats at the shelter are separated in rooms with food lying next to the door. As a volunteer, you will have to make sure the right type of food is fed to the cat, which should be associated with the room they're in.

socializing - socializing with the cats will be one part of how you, as a volunteer, will maintain their mental health. You will be responsible for spending time with the cats from time to time to ensure that they are mentally healthy. This will include playing with them with the toys provided at the shelter, and cuddling with them to keep them comfortable at the shelter and around people in general. Some cats are shy and will require a different type of socializing to make them comfortable being there. This will also involve spending time with them, but with smaller steps to help them socialize.

Grooming - Keeping the cats well-groomed will be how you, as a volunteer, will maintain the cats' physical health. This will include brushing them to keep them and their room free of hair clutter. You will also have to clip their claws so that they don't overgrow and get too long for them.

By caring for the cats at Eden, you will ensure that they will remain healthy and happy. While you are here at Eden, you will basically be their babysitter for the time that you are here.
