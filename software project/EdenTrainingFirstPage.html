shelter tasks

One portion of your responsibilities as a volunteer here will be helping the coordinators with the shelter tasks. These tasks will involve various cleaning chores to keep the shelter in good condition. You will also have to help in caring for the cats that are at the shelter. These tasks will include feeding, taking care of their hygienic needs, and socializing with them. Doing these tasks will make you a great volunteer and a great addition to the shelter.

Basic Five

The first portion of the task that is required is called the basic five. The basic Five are the tasks that will have to be done daily. These tasks will be done in the morning and the afternoon to maintain the shelter and the cats. Here is a rundown on those tasks:

Feeding - The first and second of the basic five will be feeding and watering the cats. In the shelter, there will be a room door that has cat food placed near it. As a volunteer, you would want to make sure the cats are fed daily using the food near the door. You will do this by scooping 3/4th or 75% of the food scooper that's provided and place it in whichever cat it is supposed to go to. To give the cats water, you will have to rinse the bowls out and refill them halfway with water. This will be done in the morning and in the afternoon.

Scooping - The third task in the basic five will be scooping litter. Each cat will have their own litter box that will need new litter inside every day to keep them uninfected.

Sweeping - the fourth step in the basic five will be sweeping around the shelter and the cats' room to keep it tidy and clean for anyone visiting and the coordinators.

Love - the last task in the basic five will be providing love to the cats at the shelter. This will be a combination of the other steps, but more specifically for the cats. This will include socializing with them and taking care of their hygienic needs. More about these can be found on the cat care page.
