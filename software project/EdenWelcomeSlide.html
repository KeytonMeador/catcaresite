Hello New Volunteer

Welcome to Eden's Animal Haven Training Section. Now that you are a volunteer, it is important that you know what your job here is as a volunteer and how to properly do that job. This Orientation is a way to teach you the basics of what is expected here and the procedures that we use to conduct those expectations. Some of those expectations will be:

Making sure the shelter's propley is taken care of and is cleaned.

Making sure the cats are cared for and loved.

The coordinators will have aid in extra stuff going on at the shelter.
